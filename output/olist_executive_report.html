Critical finding: Olist is functioning as a pure customer-acquisition engine with no retention flywheel. At the current 3% repeat rate, each new customer is essentially a one-shot revenue event worth R$ 159,86. If the repeat rate reaches 6%, still far below any e-commerce benchmark, incremental annual revenue is +R$ 498.000, at an assumed retention cost of R$ 40 per converted customer (net ROI: +R$ 386.000).
Key Findings
!
Near-zero retention.
97% of 93.350 unique customers never placed a second order. The platform lacks any structural mechanism (loyalty, re-engagement email, or personalisation) to bring buyers back.
▼
Late delivery destroys satisfaction.
6,8% of delivered orders arrived after the estimated date. Late orders average 2,30 ★ versus 4,27 ★ for on-time orders, a 1,97-star gap that directly suppresses repeat intent.
★
Order value predicts return likelihood.
Higher order value and installment use are the strongest predictors of repeat purchase in the ML model (AUC 0,61). Premium buyers are most worth retaining.
◆
Geographic concentration amplifies risk.
São Paulo generates 42% of orders. North/Northeast states (AL, MA, CE) have late rates above 13% and average delivery beyond 20 days, the worst satisfaction scores in the country.
R$
Revenue concentrated in 3 categories.
Health & Beauty, Watches & Gifts, and Bed/Bath Table account for 26% of total product revenue. Office Furniture, a high-ticket category, carries the lowest satisfaction of any major category at 3,63 ★.

Launch a post-purchase re-engagement email sequence targeting customers within 30 to 60 days of first delivery. Personalise by top category. Critical Retention rate +1 to 3 pp, +R$ 166K to 498K/yr

Data Quality Decisions
S
Scope: delivered orders only.
2.971 orders (3,0%) in non-delivered status (cancelled, shipped, processing) excluded from retention analysis. A customer cannot decide to return before their order arrives.
★
Missing review scores: 1.932 orders.
Imputed as 3,0 (neutral). These orders received no review request response, neither demonstrably positive nor negative. Neutral avoids model bias in either direction.
8
Missing delivery dates: 8 orders.
Both purchase and delivery timestamps absent, not imputable. Excluded from ML model only; included in EDA counts.
F
Freight ratio capped at 99th percentile.
Extreme outliers (freight > 4× product price) would dominate the ML model. Cap applied; original values retained in export CSV for Power BI.
ID
customer_unique_id used, not customer_id.
Dataset documentation confirms customer_id changes per order (privacy anonymisation). Repeat rate is computed on customer_unique_id, the true user identifier.
